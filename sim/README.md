Attached please find a rough estimation on signal level and shape for the NSLS-II SB BPM study, assuming an absorptive Gaussian LPF with f3dB=117 MHz. This estimation is based on numerical simulated signals for the ESR pickup, 6 mm button diameter in a 80x36 mm chamber, and beam, 7 mm RMS bunch length, bunch charge 1 nC. Also included is ~30 meter coaxial cable, 3/8" Heliax.
I expect the signals from the NSLS-II BPM buttons a bit higher, due to the smaller chamber and perhaps 1/2" Heliax cable, probably within a factor 2 or so. Please note, low-passing, i.e. 'stretching' the signal to ~10 ns means we discard most of the high-frequency signal components, therefore the peak voltage is substantially reduced, and I guess we may need some kind of gain stage (+20 dB ?!) to adapt to the FSR of the ADCs.
